Fitness

Certification

Pole Fitness is one of the fastest growing forms of fitness today. In fact there are thousands of Pole Dancing Schools all over the world, offering classes to women, men even children!

Do you enjoy working out? Have you been working as an exotic dancer and want to use your career skills to your advantage? Or perhaps you have been taking pole dancing classes and have been entertaining the idea of teaching one day.

Having a pole dance instructors certification under your belt is an important step in your journey. I have written the Pole Dance Instructors Certification, which is certification program designed to provide the instruction and guidance to encourage a fun fitness class in a safe and supportive environment.

Programs

Basic Certification

Learn Pole Dancing, Levels 1, 2, 3 and 4

  • Four levels of Choreography - Complete Routines
  • 70 Pole, Dance and Floor Moves, plus Transitions
  • Receive 16-20 hours of dance instruction to learn the moves, and choreography as well as appropriate methods for teaching
  • Open discussion for Q & A
  • A complete study and reference guide detailing all of the pole, dance and floor moves learned
  • Detailed information on how the muscles are getting a workout from each move
  • Unlimited email contact after the completion of the course to answer any questions regarding the choreography or teaching methods
  • Basic documents for liability, and sign-in information
  • Membership to Pole Fitness Studio
  • Certificate of Completion
